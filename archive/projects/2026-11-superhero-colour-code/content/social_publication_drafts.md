## LinkedIn
I've revisited an old Tableau project with a new question: can you tell a comic-book hero from a villain by the colour of the costume?

I've ended up with three very different charts. One costume line-up. Two wheels of Marvel and DC colour. And five unlikely hero–villain colour twins.

Superman and Thanos both wear blue. Green Lantern and Green Goblin share green. Which is a fairly awkward outcome for the traditional comic-book dress code.

The dataset is a carefully selected 24-character illustration, not a claim about every comic ever printed. And I rather like the surprises.

The three-chart story will be at coffeetableviz.com once the final sources are signed off.

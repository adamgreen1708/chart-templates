## Unresolved / disputed: original artwork reuse
- **London Museum** credits its image © London Museum; its collection entry flags an image-licensing route.
- A **Bridgeman Images** catalogue page for the 1933 Beck image calls it *in copyright* and requires permissions due diligence.
- **Wikimedia Commons** displays a 1933 version as public domain under a UK Government/Crown copyright rationale. This conflicts with commercial and museum rights notices and is not enough on its own to clear reuse for Coffeetableviz.
- **Decision:** link to original museum-held object for educational context; do not download, host, inline/hotlink or closely trace the original 1933 artwork until rights for the exact image are checked. A simplified original editorial teaching diagram using its own shapes and verified modern data does not purport to be the 1933 artefact.

## Another discrepancy not used
- TfL archival guide attributes **ten guineas** for the design, folders and artwork, whereas English Heritage mentions a reported **five guineas** for the original. Scope and provenance differ; no payment claim in editorial copy or visual.

## Historical integrity safeguards
1. Cite Beck's specific **1933** pocket issue; call 1931 the proposal/date of initial design.
2. Distinguish **geographic location**, **schematic position** and **station topology**. Schematic edge lengths must not read as journey time or geographic kilometres.
3. Network comparison must have documented **same station identities and line connections** on both panels. If using current network data, label period; never pass it off as the 1933 network.
4. Station coordinates and adjacency require direct sources and reproducible parsing. No invented lines, station names, or interchanges.
5. Original design uses diamonds for interchange; newer Tube maps need not preserve this symbol exactly.
6. Tube network is not primarily quantitative data visualization. It is an iconic **topological information encoding**.
7. Do not repeat small details that have uncertain provenance (unresolved payment figure or first-ever global invention claims).

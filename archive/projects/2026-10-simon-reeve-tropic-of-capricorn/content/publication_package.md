# Publication package — Simon Reeve's Tropic of Capricorn

## Editorial recommendation

Lead with the tension between a perfectly straight geographic idea and the messy reality of following it around the planet.

**Approved editorial takeaway:** one invisible line creates a very crooked journey.

## Published headline

## Standfirst / excerpt

Simon Reeve followed the Tropic of Capricorn through 10 countries and three continents. The line is straight. The journey was anything but.

## Feature-image metaphor

**A perfectly straight latitude line versus the route needed to follow it.**

Three abstract black/charcoal land masses represent Africa, Australia and South America. A rigid dashed horizontal line crosses all three, while a thick winding black route repeatedly veers above and below it. The journey ends on a single muted-red marker.

The image therefore communicates the exact story tension — straight idea, crooked journey — rather than a generic travel scene.

## Feature-image construction brief

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: square coffeetableviz editorial feature image. Follow `spec/feature_image_rules.md`.

Create a deterministic square SVG on the house light-grey `#F3F4F6` background. Use three abstract black/charcoal land masses across the canvas. Draw one perfectly straight dashed grey latitude line horizontally through them. Overlay one thick black journey path that repeatedly zig-zags above and below the straight reference line as it travels left to right. End the route at one muted-red `#C44E52` circular marker as the only colour accent.

Strong negative space, crop-safe and readable at thumbnail size. No text, map labels, faces, Simon Reeve likeness, flags, logos, watermarks or copyrighted character imagery.

## Source verification

- Simon Reeve's official biography supports the early-career account: leaving school without qualifications, time on the dole, charity-shop and shelf-stacking work, newspaper post-boy work and investigative journalism in his early twenties.
- His official biography states he has travelled to more than 130 countries and made more than 100 television programmes.
- Simon Reeve's official television page gives the Capricorn country sequence from Namibia through Botswana, South Africa, Mozambique, Madagascar, Australia, Chile, Argentina, Paraguay and Brazil.
- Penguin describes *Tropic of Capricorn* as a 23,000-mile journey through Africa, Australia and South America and explicitly frames the book around environmental change, poverty and globalisation.
- Chart 2 coordinates are approximate and documented in `content/source_notes.md`.
- The Ground/Air/Water/Animal grouping is a coffeetableviz editorial classification, not wording attributed to Reeve.

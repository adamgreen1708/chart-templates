# Source notes

## Route spine

The country and place sequence is based on Adam's supplied Tropic of Capricorn notes:

Namibia → Botswana → South Africa → Mozambique → Madagascar → Australia → Chile → Argentina → Paraguay → Brazil.

The supplied notes also name the places used in the longitude chart, including Windhoek, Gaborone, Kruger National Park, Bazaruto, Toliara, Ilakaka, Fianarantsoa, Manakara, Wittenoom, Uluru, Alice Springs, Queensland, Heron Island, San Pedro de Atacama, Wichí territory, Mbaracayú, Ciudad del Este, Iguaçu Falls, São Paulo and Ubatuba.

## Journey distance and framing

Penguin describes *Tropic of Capricorn* as a 23,000-mile journey through Africa, Australia and South America.

## Transport modes

Simon Reeve's official Tropic of Capricorn page names travel by van, car, train, boat, horseback, helicopter and plane, plus a road train in Australia. Those eight named modes are grouped for Chart 3 as:

- Ground: van, car, train, road train
- Air: helicopter, plane
- Water: boat
- Animal: horseback

The four-family grouping is a coffeetableviz editorial classification, not wording attributed to Reeve.

## Coordinates

Chart 2 uses approximate public coordinates for the named places. Sources include Wikidata, the Australian Bureau of Meteorology, Geoscience Australia, the Northern Territory Place Names Register and OpenStreetMap/GeoNames-derived place records.

The final chart explicitly states that positions are approximate and that the vertical scale is exaggerated 3×. It is not presented as a GPS reconstruction.

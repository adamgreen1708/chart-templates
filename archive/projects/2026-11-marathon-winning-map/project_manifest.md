# Marathon majors: the winning map got smaller

**Status:** Draft for editorial and visual review — not approved for live publication.

**Pinned dataset:** `data/marathon-majors/`, commit `999b9b57c2cf61a1222bb4f08a4bdd885898e09a`; upstream Shorts in Sports pinned in the dataset README.

**Editorial claim:** The Kenyan/Ethiopian share of **women's winners** across these six historical major races rose from 22/51 (43.1%) in 2000–09 to 50/53 (94.3%) in 2010–19. For men the same matched-race share rose from 39/51 (76.5%) to 48/53 (90.6%). The shorter 2020–25 period is 26/29 (89.7%) for women and 28/29 (96.6%) for men.

## Three charts

1. `01_women_2000s.py` — country-by-country baseline for women, 2000s
2. `02_women_2010s.py` — concentration on Kenya and Ethiopia by 2010s
3. `03_share_by_sex.py` — synthesis: the women's shift far exceeds the men's

All charts run through `src/render_538.py` with `render_profile="social_square"`. Source rows stay intact; derived CSVs are generated by `scripts/build_chart_views.py`.

## Method and caveats

- Six **historical** Majors only: Tokyo, Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago, NYC. Not a complete set of today's Majors.
- Source year 2000–2025; 2026 is excluded as several entries have notes requiring external confirmation.
- Join men and women by race and source year. Keep only paired rows with `Event_Status=Completed`, `Result_Status=Not independently checked`, and nonblank nationality. Exclude virtual, cancelled, explicitly flagged and disqualification-mentioned entries. This produces 133 paired race-years, with period denominators 51, 53 and 29.
- **Not independently checked** does not mean independently verified. These charts describe the supplied Shorts in Sports-based data; no claim of exhaustive official championship results is made.
- Country means `Nationality` as supplied, not athlete birthplace, ethnicity, participation, all runners or medal placings.
- Tokyo's entry starts in 2007, and periods differ in race coverage. The 2020–25 slice is shorter and affected by disruptions.
- The pink/red highlight consistently marks Kenya/Ethiopia among women's winners; teal is men's two-country share in chart 3. Grey is context.
- Charts 1 and 2 use identical 0–33 x scales for valid visual comparison.
- Full QA: inspect exact 1600px renderer PNG and 390px / 320px reductions, margins, labels, source credit, and title wraps before approval.

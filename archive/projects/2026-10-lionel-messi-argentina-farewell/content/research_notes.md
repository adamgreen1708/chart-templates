# Lionel Messi Argentina farewell — research notes

Accessed: 7 October 2026

## Editorial hypothesis

Messi's Argentina career saved its most successful chapter until the end: extraordinary longevity, four senior-final defeats before 2021, then four trophies in four consecutive senior finals, while his international scoring rate accelerated rather than faded.

Farewell reconciliation:

Therefore the final annual row is 2026 = 12 appearances, 11 goals. Adding the farewell to the pre-match National-Football-Teams spine reconciles the full career to 208 / 126.

## Source conflict note

AFA's 6 October 2026 farewell statistics article is internally inconsistent, variously printing 207/125 and 207/126 despite the Benin match being complete:
https://www.afa.com.ar/es/posts/messi-207-partidos-126-goles-y-una-historia-para-siempre-con-la-seleccion

For final career totals this project therefore uses the reconciled match spine plus Opta, Reuters and FIFA post-match totals. AFA remains useful for the four senior-title list and tournament context, but its headline totals are not used as the controlling career count.

## Scope

Senior Argentina A internationals only. Youth internationals and the 2008 Olympic tournament are excluded from the cap/goal story. The Finalissima is included in the finals chart because it is one of Messi's four senior Argentina trophies.

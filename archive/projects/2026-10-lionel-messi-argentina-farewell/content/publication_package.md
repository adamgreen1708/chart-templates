# Publication package — Lionel Messi Argentina farewell

## Editorial recommendation

Lead with the late-career reversal, not a generic retirement tribute.

**Approved editorial takeaway:** the ending changed the shape of Messi's Argentina story.

The charts show three linked things: a surprisingly busy final six years, the complete reversal from four lost senior finals to four consecutive trophy-winning finals, and a sustained late-career lift in scoring rate.

## Proposed headline

**The ending changed the story**

## Standfirst / excerpt

Lionel Messi finished his Argentina career with 208 caps and 126 goals. The striking part is how much of the story arrived late: 66 caps, four trophies and a much higher scoring rate from 2021 onwards.

## Story sequence

1. **208 caps. Plenty left for the final act.**  
   Annual Argentina appearances from 2005–2026. Sixty-six of 208 caps came from 2021 onwards, including a career-high 16 appearances in 2021.

2. **Four finals lost. Then four won.**  
   Four senior-final defeats from 2007–16 were followed by four consecutive trophy-winning finals from 2021–24. The 2026 World Cup runner-up is retained as the final coda.

3. **The scoring lift wasn't a blip.**  
   Annual goals per appearance show the late-career rise in context. The 2005–20 average is 0.50; the 2021–26 average is 0.83.

## Chart captions and alt text

### Chart 1 — 208 caps. Plenty left for the final act.

**Caption:** Messi made 208 senior Argentina appearances. Sixty-six came from 2021 onwards, including a career-high 16 in 2021.

**Alt text:** Bar chart of Lionel Messi's annual Argentina appearances from 2005 to 2026. A divider marks 2021, with 66 of his 208 caps coming from 2021 onwards. The career-high 16 appearances in 2021 are annotated and the final 12 appearances in 2026 are highlighted.

### Chart 2 — Four finals lost. Then four won.

**Caption:** Four lost senior finals from 2007–16 were followed by four trophy-winning finals from 2021–24. The 2026 World Cup runner-up broke the winning streak.

**Alt text:** Two-row chart of the nine senior finals Lionel Messi reached with Argentina. The first four finals from 2007 to 2016 are grey runner-up points. The next four from 2021 to 2024 are red champion points. The 2026 World Cup is shown as a final grey runner-up point.

### Chart 3 — The scoring lift wasn't a blip

**Caption:** Messi's scoring rate averaged 0.50 goals per appearance through 2020 and 0.83 from 2021 onwards. The late-career lift lasted across several seasons rather than appearing as one isolated spike.

**Alt text:** Line chart of Lionel Messi's Argentina goals per appearance by calendar year from 2005 to 2026. The earlier high is 1.33 in 2012, while 2022 reaches 1.29 and 2026 ends at 0.92. Reference lines show averages of 0.50 for 2005–20 and 0.83 for 2021–26.

## Feature-image metaphor

**A long career line that changes trajectory late.**

A single black/charcoal horizontal path crosses most of a square light-grey canvas with a restrained, almost flat rhythm. Along the earlier stretch sit four thin, empty cup silhouettes — near-misses rather than trophies. In the final third, the line turns sharply upwards and passes four solid black cup silhouettes clustered close together, before ending at one muted-red circular marker.

The metaphor carries the specific story: a long career whose summary changed late, with repeated near-misses giving way to concentrated success and a strong final stretch.

## Feature-image prompt / construction brief

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: square coffeetableviz editorial feature image. Follow `spec/feature_image_rules.md`.

Create a square stencil / screen-print editorial illustration on the house light-grey `#F3F4F6` background. Use a strong black-and-charcoal horizontal career line travelling from left to right across most of the image. Keep its early path restrained and mostly level. Along this first two-thirds, place four simple thin-outline empty trophy cup silhouettes, spaced apart, representing repeated near-misses.

In the final third, make the career line turn visibly upward. Place four compact solid black trophy cup silhouettes along this rising section, closer together than the earlier cups, showing concentrated late success. Continue the line beyond the cups to a single muted-red `#C44E52` circular endpoint as the only colour accent.

The composition must read at thumbnail size as: long wait → late rise → concentrated success → final marker. Strong negative space. Crop-safe.

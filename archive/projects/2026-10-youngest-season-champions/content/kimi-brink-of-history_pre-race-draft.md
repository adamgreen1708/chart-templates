## Headline
Kimi Antonelli stands on the brink of F1 history

## Subtitle / standfirst
At 20, the Mercedes driver could become Formula 1's youngest-ever world champion at the US Grand Prix. But how does that compare with the champions of tennis, golf and other individual sports?

## Article

At 20, Kimi Antonelli is facing a rather unusual possibility: becoming a Formula 1 world champion while many drivers his age are still working their way towards the top of the sport.

The Mercedes driver arrives at the United States Grand Prix on pole position and with a 102-point lead over team-mate George Russell. Victory in Austin could make him the youngest champion in the sport's history. But there is a catch: Antonelli must win, Russell must finish ninth or lower, and Lewis Hamilton must finish fifth or lower. Even with a commanding championship lead, the maths is rather less straightforward than the headline.

Which raises a broader question: **how young is a young champion?**

### 1. The F1 record that keeps getting younger

Sebastian Vettel was 23 years and 134 days old when he won the 2010 Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship. That remains the record ahead of Sunday's race. Before Vettel came Lewis Hamilton at 23, Fernando Alonso at 24 and, much earlier, Emerson Fittipaldi at 25.

If Antonelli seals the title in Austin on 25 October, he would be **20 years and 61 days** old: more than three years younger than Vettel was. That's a substantial leap, not a small improvement around the edges.

### 2. Young? It depends on the sport

F1's current record feels extraordinary until you look elsewhere. Chloe Kim won the World Snowboard Tour overall ranking at 13. Martina Hingis was the WTA's year-end No. 1 at 17. Lydia Ko captured the LPGA's season-long Race to the CME Globe at 17, while Carlos Alcaraz finished 2022 as the ATP's year-end No. 1 at 19.

The point isn't that one sport is easier than another. Far from it. The remarkable thing is how differently a sporting career can develop — and how wildly the age of a season champion can vary.

### 3. What actually counts as a championship?

There's an important qualification. We aren't counting one-off Wimbledon titles, individual golf majors or Olympic gold medals. Every achievement here reflects performance across multiple events over a season.

Even then, the route to the top varies. Formula 1 counts race points across the season. Tennis recognises its year-end No. 1 through tour rankings. Some sports combine a regular season with a final championship event. Same broad question, rather different ways of answering it.

### The next finish line

Antonelli might leave Austin with the record tonight, or have to wait until another race. Either way, the comparison is worth making before the chequered flag: in some sports a 20-year-old champion sounds almost routine; in Formula 1 it would make history.

# Mini-post angle: Every Bowie album has a shape

## Recommended headline

**Every Bowie album has a shape**

## Alternative headlines

- **The average Bowie track hides the interesting bit**
- **The famous song is not always the typical song**
- **Distribution is the story: Bowie by the minute**

## Standfirst

An album average gives you one number. Plot every Bowie track and the catalogue looks much less tidy: long tails, short interludes, famous outliers, and albums where mean and median tell different stories.

## Hook

The previous Bowie story used album medians because medians are robust and easy to compare.

Useful. But incomplete.

A median collapses an album into one point.

So this time: **plot every track.**

## Rising insights

### Some albums are tightly packed; others are stretched

- *Space Oddity*: 8:52 shortest-to-longest range.
- *1. Outside*: 6:22.
- *Station to Station*: 6:13.
- *Blackstar*: 5:17.

### Mean and median can disagree for a reason

- *Blackstar*: mean 5:53, median 4:52 (+61 sec).
- *The Man Who Sold the World*: mean 4:34, median 4:00 (+34 sec).
- *Space Oddity*: mean 4:38, median 4:09 (+29 sec).
- *Low*: mean 3:32, median 3:03 (+29 sec).
- *Earthling*: mean 5:26, median 5:00 (+26 sec).
- *1. Outside*: mean 3:56, median 4:22 (-26 sec).

### The famous track can sit far from the centre

- *Let's Dance* — *Let’s Dance*: 7:37 vs 4:59 album median.
- *Heroes* — *“Heroes”*: 6:10 vs 3:48 album median.
- *Station to Station* — *Golden Years*: 4:01 vs 6:02 album median.
- *Blackstar* — *Lazarus*: 6:22 vs 4:52 album median.
- *Space Oddity* — *Space Oddity*: 5:16 vs 4:09 album median.
- *Diamond Dogs* — *Rebel Rebel*: 4:34 vs 3:27 album median.

## Aha moment

**The most famous track is not necessarily the most representative track.**

On *“Heroes”* and *Let's Dance*, the album's most-streamed song is also a formal duration outlier.

On *Station to Station*, the opposite happens: *Golden Years* is the album's most-streamed song but is about two minutes shorter than the album median.

Success therefore works best as an annotation on the distribution, not as the thing driving the chart.

## TLDR

Bowie's albums have different internal shapes. Mean and median sometimes agree; sometimes a long title track, an instrumental or a short segue pulls them apart. And the song people stream most can live near the centre — or way out on the edge.

**Distribution is the story.**

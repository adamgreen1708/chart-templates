# Brad Pitt quotability — blog copy notes

## Quote examples are required in the article

The published post should not present quote counts as abstract numbers only. Use a small number of **short, verified examples** from the roles being discussed so the reader can feel the difference between the characters.

Keep the excerpts brief and use them as editorial texture rather than reproducing long dialogue.

Recommended examples verified against IMDb quote pages on 23 September 2026:

- **Fury — Don 'Wardaddy' Collier:** “Ideals are peaceful. History is violent.”
- **Fight Club — Tyler Durden:** “The things you own end up owning you.”
- **Moneyball — Billy Beane:** “How can you not be romantic about baseball?”
- **Troy — Achilles:** “You gave me peace in a lifetime of war.”
- **F1 — Sonny Hayes:** “Hope is not a strategy.”
- **Ocean's Eleven — Rusty Ryan:** “Been practicing that speech, haven't you?”

## How to use them

### Opening / Fury hook

The opening should connect Adam watching *Fury* to the question behind the analysis. Use Wardaddy's short line as an immediate example rather than opening with the chart methodology.

Suggested shape:

Adam is watching *Fury*. Wardaddy drops a line such as “Ideals are peaceful. History is violent.” That prompts the question: where does this character sit among Brad Pitt's most quotable film roles?

### Chart 1 discussion

Do not simply list the top five counts. Contrast voice as well as volume.

Examples:
- Tyler Durden's “The things you own end up owning you.” helps explain why *Fight Club* feels naturally quotable.
- Billy Beane's “How can you not be romantic about baseball?” shows a completely different kind of memorable line.
- Achilles' “You gave me peace in a lifetime of war.” gives the ranking a more dramatic register.
- Sonny Hayes' “Hope is not a strategy.” is almost designed to become a repeatable one-liner.

The point is not that these individual excerpts caused the IMDb counts. They simply make the ranking tangible.

### Chart 2 discussion

Use *Fury* as the bridge between quote count and rating:

Wardaddy has 40 IMDb quote entries and *Fury* carries a 7.6 IMDb rating. The line “Ideals are peaceful. History is violent.” is a useful example of the compact, self-contained dialogue that makes the role easy to quote.

Avoid implying that quote quality, quote count and film rating are causally linked.

### Chart 3 discussion

Use Rusty's dry “Been practicing that speech, haven't you?” as a small flavour note before showing how his quote count falls from *Ocean's Eleven* to the two sequels.

## Copy rule

- Prefer 3–5 short quote examples across the whole post.
- Do not reproduce long speeches.
- Attribute every quote to character + film in surrounding prose.
- Keep the article focused on the data story, with quotes supporting the analysis rather than replacing it.
- Re-verify exact wording against the source before final publication.

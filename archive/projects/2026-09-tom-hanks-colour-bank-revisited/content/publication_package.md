A fresh IMDb snapshot turns Tom Hanks’ filmography into something more interesting than a ranking. His ratings record changed sharply in 1992—and one familiar cowboy kept the run alive for another 31 years.

## Chart captions and alt text

### Chart 1 — Hanks found another gear in 1992

**Caption:** The 14 rated narrative films in the 1992–2002 IMDb principal-credit run average 7.79; 10 rate 7.5 or higher.

**Alt text:** Scatter plot of IMDb film rating by year for 61 Tom Hanks narrative-film principal credits from 1980 to 2026. Films from 1992 to 2002 are highlighted in blue. Forrest Gump is the highest-rated at 8.8. A dashed line marks the overall median of 6.9.

### Chart 2 — Ten hits in eleven years

**Caption:** None of 15 films before the run rate 7.5 or higher, compared with 10 of 14 from 1992–2002 and 6 of 32 afterwards.

**Alt text:** Dot plot comparing the share of Tom Hanks narrative films rated 7.5 or higher across three periods. The 1992–2002 run is highlighted at 71%, compared with 0% before and 19% after.

### Chart 3 — Woody outlasted the golden run

**Caption:** All five Toy Story films rate above Tom Hanks’ overall film median of 6.9; their franchise median is 7.9 across 1995–2026.

**Alt text:** Line chart of IMDb ratings for five Toy Story films from 1995 to 2026. Ratings are 8.3, 7.9, 8.3, 7.6 and 7.4. Every point sits above the dashed Tom Hanks career median of 6.9; Toy Story 5 is highlighted in red.

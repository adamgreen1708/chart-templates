# How the Premier League unfolded — 2025–26

**Category:** Sport  
**Status:** editorial draft for interactive Resource; not a published blog post.

Arsenal took the Premier League title with **85 points**. Manchester City finished seven behind. That's the ending.

But there is more to a season than the final table.

In my original 2020–21 Tableau project, I wanted to see the months — and the home and away results — as the season happened. Not just a line racing upwards, but wins, draws and defeats you could actually count.

This new version brings that idea into Coffeetableviz.

Choose a club and watch its calendar change. Red circles mark wins, light grey marks draws and darker grey defeats. Home results sit above the month labels; away results below. Tap a dot to find out who played, what happened and when. The club's home and away points, goals and form sit beside the dots.

Then look down to the final-points rail. All twenty clubs share the same 0–90 scale, so you can jump from a champion to a relegated side and see a very different pattern.

The points might have come from the same competition. The journeys rarely looked the same.

## A note on the numbers

The chart includes all **380 full-time Premier League fixtures** played from **15 August 2025 to 24 May 2026**, from [Football-Data.co.uk](https://www.football-data.co.uk/mmz4281/2526/E0.csv) via a frozen, traceable GitHub snapshot. Each result is dated and counted once for each club, with 19 home and 19 away games.

The months are the actual fixture calendar months, rather than designated matchweeks. Home and away points are calculated as three per win and one per draw. All twenty final point totals match the official end-of-season table.

**TL;DR:** Twenty clubs, 380 matches, ten months, and one simple question: how did the season unfold?

*Simple charts. Clear stories.*

## LinkedIn post

Simon Pegg has spent two decades bouncing between Cornetto pubs, starships and impossible missions.

The ratings pattern is rather less chaotic.

Using IMDb's non-commercial datasets, I found 33 released principal-actor movies with at least 1,000 votes. Twelve belong to three recurring groups: six Mission: Impossible films, three Star Trek films and three films directed by Edgar Wright.

Those 12 average **7.43** on IMDb.

The other 21 average **6.09**.

That is a **1.34-point gap**—and the three recurring groups are remarkably close to one another: Star Trek 7.53, Edgar Wright 7.50 and Mission: Impossible 7.35.

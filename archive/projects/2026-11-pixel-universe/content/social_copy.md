## LinkedIn — primary

A superhero in just 256 squares?

After exploring who wears the hero colours, I ended up down another small rabbit hole: could those same palettes work as pixel art?

The result: 12 tiny hero and villain portraits, from Superman's red-and-blue world to the Joker's rather more purple wardrobe.

The fun bit is that they're not just pictures. Each design opens in my Pixel Art Studio, where you can remix it and save your own version. A cape is optional.

A little data, a little design, and probably too much time thinking about individual squares.

Take a look at coffeetableviz.com/resources/pixel-universe/ once it's live.

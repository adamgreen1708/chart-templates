# Approved-story feature illustration prompt and image QA

*Publication staging, 19 November 2026. Awaiting approval of cover illustration; article and three chart images approved.*

## Visual metaphor
**A five-film phenomenon and a career of connections, culminating in a human Bond handover.** Five generic film-frame icons on a looping filmstrip lead via six cast-connection paths to two anonymous formal-jacket silhouettes. One connecting path, associated with the real earlier *Remember Me* co-star Pierce Brosnan, is muted red. It is explicitly a metaphor for a career path and a former actor welcoming the new 007; it is **not** a claim that any co-star relationship caused the casting.

## Image construction prompt (original deterministic SVG)

Create a square, screen-print/stencil illustration with the exact **#F3F4F6** light-grey background. Draw the filmstrip in charcoal/black with five small repeating frame motifs, then six thin lines towards two *faceless*, generic, unidentifiable formal-jacket figures. Let one of those lines be the sole **#C44E52** muted-red accent. Keep ample negative space; no readable words or numbers, actor likenesses, faces, weapons, film stills, franchise logos, watermarks or gun-barrel motif. Strong editorial silhouette legible at mobile card size. Original symbolic SVG geometry, not a reproduction of licensed franchise art.

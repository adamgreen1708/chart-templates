I expected a Brad Pitt film list to turn into a ranking.

It didn't.

Across 54 films in an IMDb list, the 14 films under 110 minutes average 6.09. The 19 films at 130+ minutes average 7.59.

Then two more patterns appeared:

- David Fincher's three films average 8.40 — the best of the repeat directors.
- 1995–99 is the strongest rolling five-year run: seven films averaging 7.63.

Not causation. Just a much more interesting story than “here are his top ten films”.

<section class="prose-page shell-narrow">
  <p class="lede">coffeetableviz is where I explore the numbers hiding inside sport, film, culture and everyday life.</p>

  <p>I am an Analytics lead, avid Tableau enthusiast (and previous Ambassador). I spend my working life helping people see and understand data, then come here to make charts about the things people discuss around a coffee table.</p>

  <h2>The rule</h2>

  <p>Every story should earn its chart. The data is checked, the visual is deliberately simple and the writing gets to the point.</p>

  <blockquote><p>simple charts clear stories.</p></blockquote>

  <h2>How it works</h2>

  <p>The dataset, analysis, chart configuration, rendered output and finished article all live together in GitHub. That means a chart is not just an image: its source and decisions remain attached.</p>

  <p>This prototype is the first step in bringing the coffeetableviz archive into that same system.</p>
</section>

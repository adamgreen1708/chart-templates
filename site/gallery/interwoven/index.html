Every mark has meaning.

Read left to right in chronological fixture order. Clubs are arranged by final league position. Home games sit above each club’s thread, away games below. Sphere area represents goals scored, with a visible minimum for zero. Only victories have an outer halo; draws have dashed connections. Brighter connections indicate more match goals, and illuminated ribbons identify winning runs of five or more consecutive club fixtures.

The artwork records fixtures at equal horizontal spacing, rather than equal intervals of time. Same-date fixtures follow source-file order.

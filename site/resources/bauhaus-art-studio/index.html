05 / Compose the artwork

Same numbers. Different story.

Try a composition, then change the shapes, colours and ordering. Every solid mark keeps an area encoding you can explain.

Mixed shapes follow the category when selected, otherwise repeat decoratively. Four palette colours repeat when there are more than four categories.

The min–max option exaggerates small differences: the poster will label this clearly. Original values appear when you tap a mark.

Form follows data. Pixel Art gives you squares to paint. Truchet gives you a rule to repeat. Bauhaus Art Studio adds circles, triangles, colour and — if you want — your own numbers. In Data Art mode, the solid shapes are measured rather than arbitrary: their filled areas represent the values when the zero-based proportional scale is selected. Load a local CSV or a public dataset from the Coffeetableviz GitHub repository on main. Larger CSVs can be explored with their first 64 rows; this limit is shown rather than hidden. Change the composition, shape family, colour rule and sorting, then make the headline, explanation and source line your own. The zero-based proportional area option preserves actual magnitude, while the optional contrast scale is explicitly labelled as min–max. Turn optional shape labels on to show the label, value, both, or displayed index; cramped labels are suppressed rather than colliding with their neighbours. The rest is an exercise in creative freedom. Inspired by Running in Shapes and the original Hidden Miles Tableau artwork.

The table gives away the ending. The months tell the story.

Arsenal were champions with 85 points, seven clear of Manchester City. But a final league table doesn't tell you whether those points were won at home or away, or whether a run of red win markers arrived in December rather than April.

This version returns to the idea behind the original Tableau visual: each team's season as a little calendar of results. Hover over a dot with a mouse, or tap it on your phone. Choose another club, and the monthly pattern changes; the shared finishing-points scale stays put.

A note on the data

All 380 Premier League matches played between 15 August 2025 and 24 May 2026 are included. Results were taken from Football-Data.co.uk using a pinned source snapshot; final points were checked against the official league table.

The top display groups each club's results by calendar month and venue. Each W/D/L circle is one completed match, ordered by date within its month and venue. Summary points award three for a win and one for a draw. The bottom display uses the same final points for every club on a shared 0–90 scale. Short club labels stand in for the original crests while we establish a reusable and licensed asset set.

03 / BEHIND THE LINES

What exactly are we looking at?

The horizontal axis is each club's own match number, not the shared calendar date or an official Premier League matchweek. That keeps all 20 clubs aligned by games played when fixtures are postponed. The vertical axis is cumulative league points: three for a win, one for a draw, none for a defeat.

Results comprise 380 full-time Premier League fixtures between 15 August 2025 and 24 May 2026, sourced from Football-Data.co.uk's 2025–26 results via a pinned source snapshot. The final table has been cross-checked against the Premier League's official table. The archived CSV, calculation script and rendered JSON live together in the Coffeetableviz repository.

This interaction is inspired by the original 2020–21 Season Unfolded Tableau project. This is a new interpretation, rather than a confirmed pixel-for-pixel recreation of the Tableau dashboard.

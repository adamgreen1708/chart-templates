Behind the pixels

Inspired by the data, not measured from comic art.

Each character's main, supporting and accent colours come from a hand-picked, qualitative study of 24 comic costumes. They're descriptive judgements, not fabric-area percentages. The 16×16 shapes, faces and outlines are playful artistic additions. Some costume versions and colour choices remain uncertain.

This is an unofficial creative experiment, with no affiliation to Marvel or DC. Read the original colour story or start a fresh pixel drawing.

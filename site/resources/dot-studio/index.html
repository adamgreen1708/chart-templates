How to recreate this in Tableau
  1. Open the exported dots.csv as a text file.
  2. Put X on Columns and Y on Rows, continuous from 0 to Grid_Size.
  3. Use Circle marks with Point_ID on Detail.
  4. Put Size_Area on Size and Colour_Group on Colour, then apply the colour key.
  5. Use a square canvas and hide the axes.
In halftone mode the CSV contains separate Cyan, Magenta, Yellow and Black marks, even where they overlap. Tableau may not reproduce the browser’s multiply-blended ink colours exactly; it also rescales mark sizes.
The halftone treatment is a screen-print-inspired browser simulation, not a colour-managed CMYK press proof. Uploaded images stay on your device; drafts last for this session.

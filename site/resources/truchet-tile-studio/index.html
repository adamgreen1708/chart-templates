The trick is delightfully small. Each square is divided diagonally into a pale and a dark triangle. Every rotation changes what happens next door. Link mirrored edits to see a small change echo across the design, or slide towards chaos and watch structure dissolve. Repeated across a grid, those tiny decisions become something rather unexpected.

A season still being written.

The league table changes every weekend. The results above show only completed matches — the selected club's monthly home and away record, and its points earned so far.

Choose a club, then tap a result to discover the opponent, score and date. The tooltip adds cumulative points for all twenty clubs, with your club highlighted. Each line follows that club's own completed match number, not a shared calendar matchweek.

A note on the data

The initial review snapshot came from Football-Data.co.uk. Updates use the authenticated football-data.org Premier League results API. The chart footer identifies the source of the loaded snapshot, and the chart states its latest included result date rather than assuming it is current. Only completed full-time results that pass validation count. Positions are provisional; teams may have played different numbers of matches. Tied clubs are ordered on points, goal difference and goals scored; further official tie-breakers are not modelled.

Home results radiate upwards and away results downwards from each month label, earliest nearest the centre. Wins use representative club colours, draws light grey and defeats dark grey. The points rail rescales with points earned, rather than implying a final table.

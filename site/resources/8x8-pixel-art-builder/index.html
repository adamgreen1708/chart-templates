Interactive playground · Design

8×8 pixel art builder.

64 squares. Eight colours. A surprising amount of creative possibility. Make something small, save it, and share it with someone who needs a tiny distraction.

01 / Pick your palette

Choose a colour.

Tap or click a colour, then paint on the canvas. Drag to colour several squares. Tap a square in the same colour again to turn it white.

Need a starting point?

Borrow one of these. You can paint straight over it.

A small canvas, a good excuse to play

Not every visualisation needs a spreadsheet.

I first built this experiment in Tableau in January 2023, using parameters and dashboard actions to turn a data visualisation into a drawing tool. This version keeps the eight-by-eight idea, but runs directly in your browser — no Tableau required.

Your design stays on your device unless you choose to share its link. Save a sharp 1,024 × 1,024 PNG, or send the link so someone else can open the same 64 squares.

Explore my original Tableau Public builder. The first version also credited Ant Pulley's Paint Your Own Bob Ross experiment as inspiration.

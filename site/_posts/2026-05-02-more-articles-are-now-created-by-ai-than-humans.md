<h2>AI crossed the line…</h2>

<p>This one felt a bit different to write 😬</p>

<ul>
<li>The data analysis… <em>Done with ChatGPT</em>.</li>

<li>The data structuring… <em>Done with ChatGPT</em>.</li>

<li>The Python config and rendering? <em>Also ChatGPT.</em></li>

<li>The draft of this blog post? <em>Yep… ChatGPT again.</em></li>
</ul>

<p>So here’s the question: is this an AI article? 🤷🏼‍♂️</p>

<h2>The data says we’ve already crossed the line</h2>

<figure class="story-chart full-bleed"><img src="/assets/migrated/more-articles-are-now-created-by-ai-than-humans/img_7950-1.png" alt="IMG 7950" loading="lazy"></figure>

<p>According to <em>Graphite.io — “<a href="https://graphite.io/five-percent/more-articles-are-now-created-by-ai-than-humans">More Articles Are Now Created by AI Than Humans</a>”</em> something pretty simple and pretty big happened in late 2024:</p>

<p><strong>More articles on the web were classified as AI-generated than human-written</strong>. Not by a huge margin. But enough to <em>flip</em> the balance.</p>

<p>Back in 2020, AI content was barely visible. Just a couple of percent. Then the lines start to move, slowly at first, then very quickly after the launch of ChatGPT in November 2022. Until they met. And crossed.</p>

<h2>But here’s the part I keep coming back to</h2>

<p>This post exists because of AI.</p>

<p>It helped explore the dataset. It wrote the chart config. It generated the rendering logic. It even helped shape the narrative you’re reading now.</p>

<p>But it didn’t decide the angle, the choice to focus on the crossover or the decision to visualise it this way (<em>rather than ChatGPT’s first config render suggestion</em>)⬇️</p>

<figure class="story-chart full-bleed"><img src="/assets/migrated/more-articles-are-now-created-by-ai-than-humans/img_7946.png" alt="IMG 7946" loading="lazy"></figure>

<p>I believe, the judgement of what’s interesting still sits with the human.</p>

<h2>So… is this an AI article?</h2>

<p>It depends how you define it.</p>

<p>If more than half the words or structure came from AI… <em>maybe yes.</em></p>

<p>If the thinking, framing, and intent came from a human… <em>maybe not.</em></p>

<p>And that’s probably the real shift behind the data.</p>

<p>It’s no longer <strong>AI <em>or</em> human</strong>, It’s <strong>AI <em>with</em> human</strong>.</p>

<h2>TL;DR</h2>

<p>AI content has crossed 50%. But the more interesting question might be: what even counts as “AI content” anymore?</p>

<h2>Why do the colours work well together?</h2>

<p>The colours in the palette work well together for several reasons, the following summary focuses on colour theory and visual harmony, further reading on colour theory is available on ‘<a href="https://www.canva.com/colors/color-wheel/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">canva color wheel</a>’</p>

<pre><strong>1.Neutral Grounding</strong>: The dark grey (#484041) acts as a neutral tone, providing a stable and balanced backdrop. Neutral colours are versatile and can balance more vibrant hues, allowing them to stand out without overwhelming the viewer.<br /><strong>2.Analogous Colours</strong>: The blue (#40BCD8) and the green (#329F5B) are analogous colors, sitting next to each other on the colour wheel. Analogous colours naturally blend well together, creating a sense of harmony and cohesion.<br /><strong>3.Complementary Contrast</strong>: The soft pinkish-purple (#BC7C9C) contrasts with the green (#329F5B). These colours are complementary, adding vibrancy and dynamic energy to the palette. This contrast creates visual interest and helps important elements stand out.<br /><strong>4.Cool and Warm Balance</strong>: The palette combines cool tones (blue and green) with warm tones (pinkish-purple), achieving a balanced look. This mixture of cool and warm tones creates a harmonious and versatile palette that can evoke various emotions and moods.<br /><strong>5.Pop of Colour</strong>: The bright blue (#40BCD8) and vibrant green (#329F5B) act as accent colours, adding pops of brightness against the more subdued grey and pinkish-purple. These accent colours draw attention and add energy to the design.<br /><strong>6.Natural Harmony</strong>: The green (#329F5B) and blue (#40BCD8) evoke natural elements (like foliage and water), creating a sense of familiarity and comfort. The pinkish-purple adds a touch of elegance and warmth.<br /><br />//ChatGPT prompt<br />‘describe why these colours work well together focus on colour theory and visual harmony’</pre>

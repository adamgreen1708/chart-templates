<p>This post comes off the back of a classic “is summer here?” kind of weekend — park run, paddle boarding, cycle ride with pub stop &amp; a BBQ… the lot. Just enough to make you think the UK weather might finally be on our side.</p>

<p>So I threw the <strong>UK mean temperature</strong> dataset from the met office at my ChatGPT prompt.</p>

<h2>The heat is on….</h2>

<figure class="story-chart full-bleed"><img src="/assets/migrated/the-uks-hottest-years-are-also-shining-through/img_7907.png" alt="IMG 7907" loading="lazy"></figure>

<p>2025 is sitting right at the top of the UK’s annual mean temperature table, but what really stands out is what’s around it. It’s not a one-off, recent years are all crowded up at the top 🤔.</p>

<p>Whilst UK annual mean temperature data is interesting, I fancied a dive into the <strong>sunshine</strong> dataset ☀️.</p>

<p>Focusing on the <strong>East Anglia sunshine</strong> stats 2025 wasn’t just warm — it was bright too 😎.</p>

<p>The region recorded 1,989.6 hours of sunshine. That’s comfortably above recent standouts like 2022 and even older peaks like 2003 — and well above the long-run average of about 1,554 hours.</p>

<h2>Warm, bright, and 🩳’s at the ready</h2>

<figure class="story-chart full-bleed"><img src="/assets/migrated/the-uks-hottest-years-are-also-shining-through/img_7906.png" alt="IMG 7906" loading="lazy"></figure>

<p>The <strong>temperature story</strong> is about a pattern, those recent years showing up again and again at the top (nothing new really….global warming).</p>

<p>The <strong>sunshine story</strong> is a bit different. 2025 really stands out on its own, with a particularly bright spring doing a lot of the work.</p>

<p>Put the two together and it starts to feel a bit more real. This wasn’t just another warm year in a dataset — last year really did get off to a sunnier start, I’ll be interested to see what 2026 looks like comparatively.</p>

<h2>TL;DR</h2>

<p>2025 tops the UK temperature rankings — and in East Anglia, it was one of the brightest years on record too.</p>

<p>Same year. Two signals.</p>

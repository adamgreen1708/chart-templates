Ski jumper. Rock star. Video-game salesman. Spy. Outlaw. Airport security officer.

Taron Egerton’s film choices can feel as though somebody has been spinning a genre wheel. But a fresh look at his IMDb ratings reveals a surprisingly tidy pattern: the films based on real people are his strongest top-billed lane.

## Kingsman still sets the ceiling

First, the career shape. Among 14 released films with at least 1,000 IMDb votes, *Kingsman: The Secret Service* remains the high point at **7.7**.

That breakout has never quite been topped. Yet three later films sit in a tight group just behind it: *Eddie the Eagle* at 7.3, *Rocketman* at 7.3 and *Tetris* at 7.4.

Those three have more in common than their ratings.

## Real lives are his strongest lane

Each is tagged Biography by IMDb. Each also places Egerton first in IMDb’s principal ordering.

Compare them with his top-billed Action films and the gap is **1.13 rating points**.

<figure class="story-chart full-bleed">

  <figcaption>The three top-billed Biography films average 7.33, compared with 6.20 for the three top-billed Action films.</figcaption>

The Biography trio averages **7.33**. The Action trio—*Kingsman: The Golden Circle*, *Carry-On* and *Robin Hood*—averages **6.20**.

Six films do not make a universal rule. They do make a clean, reproducible pattern in this snapshot: audiences have rated Egerton’s real-life characters more consistently than his action leads.

## Two sequels. Opposite directions.

His two eligible franchises add a neat final twist.

<figure class="story-chart full-bleed">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/migrated/taron-egerton/franchises.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Slope chart comparing two Taron Egerton franchise pairs. Kingsman falls from 7.7 for the first film to 6.7 for The Golden Circle, while Sing rises from 7.1 to 7.3 for Sing 2." loading="lazy">
  <figcaption>The second <em>Kingsman</em> falls by 1.0 IMDb point; <em>Sing 2</em> rises by 0.2.</figcaption>
</figure>

*Kingsman* drops from 7.7 to 6.7. *Sing* moves the other way, from 7.1 to 7.3.

That is only two films in each series, so it is a pair of comparisons rather than a franchise trend. Still, it underlines the wider point: the famous spy breakthrough set a high bar, but the most dependable ratings have arrived when Egerton plays somebody who actually lived.

## TLDR

*Kingsman: The Secret Service* remains Taron Egerton’s highest-rated eligible film at 7.7. But his strongest repeatable lane is Biography: *Eddie the Eagle*, *Rocketman* and *Tetris* average 7.33 when he is first in IMDb’s principal ordering, compared with 6.20 for his equivalent Action films.

I first charted [Tom Hanks’ filmography]({{ '/2024/07/19/colour-bank-no-6-tom-hanks-filmography/' | relative_url }}) in 2024. Two years later, the same subject produced a much better question: when did all those familiar films turn into an extraordinary run?

## Same actor. Different question.

The original post began with a colour palette and fitted the films into a template. This time, I started with a fresh IMDb snapshot and let the pattern choose the charts.

That changes the story. This is no longer a list of Tom Hanks films from best to worst. It is a career with a very obvious gear change.

## The gear change

Across 15 films from 1980 to 1991, the average IMDb rating is **6.05**. Not one reaches 7.5.

Then comes 1992 and *A League of Their Own*. From there, the chart changes shape.

The 14 films released from 1992 to 2002 average **7.79**. Ten rate 7.5 or higher. The run includes *Philadelphia*, *Forrest Gump*, *Apollo 13*, *Toy Story*, *Saving Private Ryan*, *The Green Mile*, *Cast Away* and *Catch Me If You Can*.

## Ten in eleven

The comparison is fairly stark: none of the 15 films before the run reaches 7.5. During it, 10 of 14 do. After it, the figure drops to 6 of 32.

The densest patch arrives from 1999 to 2003. Five films in that five-year window average exactly **8.00**: *The Green Mile*, *Toy Story 2*, *Cast Away*, *Catch Me If You Can* and *Road to Perdition*.

The later career is not a collapse. Strong films keep appearing. They are simply less concentrated than they were during that remarkable run.

## Woody kept going

One thread crosses the peak and carries on for 31 years.

All five *Toy Story* films sit above Hanks’ overall career median of 6.9. Together, they have a median rating of **7.9**.

So, if the original aim was to make Friday-night film choosing slightly easier, the data has landed somewhere reassuringly familiar: Woody remains the safest bet in the toy box.

## TLDR

Tom Hanks’ film ratings changed sharply in 1992. Ten of his next 14 films rate 7.5 or higher on IMDb, compared with none of the previous 15. The broad peak eventually passed, but every *Toy Story* film has remained above his 6.9 career median.

<aside class="method-note">
  <h2>Method note</h2>
  <p>This analysis uses rated narrative movies in which Tom Hanks appears as an actor in IMDb’s principal-credit dataset. It contains 61 films from 1980 to 2026 and should not be treated as a guaranteed complete filmography. Four documentary records and six unrated or in-development movies were excluded. IMDb ratings are audience ratings for each film—not critical scores or ratings of Hanks’ individual performance. Ratings data run date: 19 September 2026.</p>

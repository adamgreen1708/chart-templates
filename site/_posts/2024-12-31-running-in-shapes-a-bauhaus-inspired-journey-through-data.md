This year I experimented with Bauhaus-inspired designs to visualise my running journey, merging data with a bold artistic style I love. Here is the thought process behind three visuals.

The first visualisation combines geometric abstraction with data, reflecting my monthly mileage in a minimalist format. The bold running figure symbolises motion and progress, while the bar chart below quantifies the journey month by month. March and April stand out as peak months.

## The Bauhaus-styled run log

The final visualisation is a heatmap of the full year, showing frequency and consistency — or lack of it — at a glance. Circles mark weekdays, squares mark weekends, dark blue records run activity and muted blue marks days without running.

## The Bauhaus influence

Bauhaus design emphasises function, simplicity and the beauty of geometric forms. Applying those ideas to data visualisation created a distinctive way to tell the story of my year, where art and data meet in something both informative and visually striking.

These visuals represent more than numbers; they celebrate creativity, motion and the timeless inspiration of Bauhaus design.

That is me done for 2024. Let’s hope my knees hold out for 2025.

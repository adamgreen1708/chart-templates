<p>A #CoffeeTableViz blog post and #TableauPublic Viz</p>

<h4>☕ Set up</h4>

<p>This visual charts Tour de France yellow jersey winners from 2005 to 2024, showcasing the rider, their nation, and their team. What begins as a timeline of champions quickly reveals deeper patterns in dominance — and a story that shifts from individuals to institutions.</p>

<h4>🎣 Hook</h4>

<p>Is the Tour de France won by the rider on the podium… or the machine behind them?</p>

<p>Over the last 20 years, victory has rarely been a solo affair. This visual digs into the dynamics of domination — and who’s really been calling the shots.</p>

<h4>📈 Rising insights</h4>

<p>A few have left serious marks.</p>

<ul>
<li>Chris Froome claimed 4 titles (2013–2017).</li>

<li>Tadej Pogačar has 3 wins (2020, 2021, 2024).</li>

<li>Jonas Vingegaard added 2 consecutive wins (2022–2023).</li>
</ul>

<p>Nations:</p>

<ul>
<li>The UK went from no wins to 6 titles in 8 years (2012–2019).</li>

<li>Slovenia, a country of just 2 million, stunned with 3 titles in 5 years.</li>

<li>France? Not a single win — the drought continues since 1985.</li>
</ul>

<p>Teams:</p>

<ul>
<li>Team Sky/Ineos dominated the 2010s: 7 wins in 8 years (2012–2019).</li>

<li>UAE Team Emirates (Pogačar) won 2020, 2021, 2024.</li>

<li>Team Jumbo-Visma (Vingegaard) took 2022 and 2023 — a back-to-back feat.</li>
</ul>

<h4>😮 Suspense</h4>

<p>But here’s the tension: since 2020, the baton keeps passing.</p>

<p>Sky’s long reign is over. UAE and Visma trade wins. The Tour is no longer anyone’s to dominate. So, is this chaos… or healthy competition?</p>

<h4>💡 Aha moment</h4>

<p>We’ve entered a new era: a power triangle.</p>

<p>No one rider, team, or nation has taken more than two consecutive wins since Froome. With Visma-Lease a Bike and UAE Team Emirates-XRG vying for supremacy, and new challengers rising.</p>

<p>And with the 2025 tour kicking off this past weekend, the next move is up for grabs — could we see Belgium’s Remco Evenepoel, USA’s Matteo Jorgensen or Spain’s Enric Mas have the legs over the next 3 weeks?, or even Geraint Thomas sign off his Tour de France journey with a podium finish?</p>

<h4>🏁 TL;DR</h4>

<p>From Sky’s iron grip to today’s tug-of-war between UAE team Emirates-XRG and Visma-Lease a Bike, Tour de France glory has shifted from single riders to team powerhouses. In 2025, the question isn’t just who will win — it’s which system will outpace the rest?</p>

## Preparation and review

Choose no more than one or two substantial new data projects each month. For each, check date evidence, IMDb/music dataset feasibility, scope, three possible charts and article-level source notes before any rendering.

A Blockbuster edition may connect to a finished article, but must pass its own exact-date, verified-quote and final-pixel approval gates. **Never invent findings just because the anniversary is approaching.**
